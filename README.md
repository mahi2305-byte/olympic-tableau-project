# Global Olympic Sports Performance Analysis

Tableau project analysing Olympic medal performance from 1992 to 2026
(Summer and Winter Games), with a Flask web page that embeds the dashboards
and story.

## Contents
- Datasets: athletes, medal_results, games_summary, alltime_country_rankings
- Tableau workbook (.twbx): 9 charts, 2 dashboards, 1 story with 6 scenes
- flask_app/: Flask app that embeds the Tableau dashboards and story
- embed_codes.txt: Tableau Public embed codes

## Key findings
- Total medals awarded ranged from 305 (2000) to 363 (2020).
- The United States (735) and China (696) lead all-time medal totals.
- South America (49.64%) and Europe/Asia (45.98%) have the highest
  average medal efficiency.
- Summer Games send about four times as many athletes as Winter Games.
